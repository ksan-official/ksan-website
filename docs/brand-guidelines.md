# KSAN Brand Guidelines

## Core palette

- Dutch Orange — `#F36C21`: primary accent, active navigation, calls to action, and key highlights.
- Frosted Pearl — `#FAFAFC`: primary page background and light surfaces.
- Slate Obsidian — `#1D1D1F`: primary text, dark surfaces, and high-contrast buttons.
- Soft Peach — `#FFDFCA`: warm supporting surfaces, gradients, and friendly highlights.
- Ice Blue — `#E8F0FE`: cool supporting surfaces, informational states, and visual balance.

These colors are available globally as named CSS custom properties in `src/app/globals.css`.

## Design direction

KSAN is both an information platform and a student community. The interface should feel simple and sophisticated while remaining approachable to a young audience.

- Prefer generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, and concise content.
- Use rounded corners and restrained motion to create a friendly character without looking playful or childish.
- Avoid rigid, overly angular layouts and dense institutional styling.
- Use Dutch Orange deliberately for actions and emphasis rather than as a large background everywhere.
- Balance warm Soft Peach with calm Ice Blue to separate information areas and community-oriented areas.
- Preserve accessible contrast with Slate Obsidian text on light colors and Frosted Pearl text on dark colors.
